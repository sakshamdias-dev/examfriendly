Privacy Policy

Last Updated : 02 April 2026

At EXAMFRIENDLY, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our students, parents, and educators. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our teaching-learning tools and online tuition services.

1. Information We Collect

  1. Personal Information
    • Registration Data: Name, email address, phone number, and age (specifically to identify student/parent roles).
    • Payment Information: For non-academic courses and tuition fees (processed via secure third-party gateways).
  2. Device And Technological Permissions

    To ensure our teaching tools function correctly, we request the following:

    • Location: To comply with regional educational regulations and suggest local tuition timings.
    • Storage: To allow students to download study materials and upload assignments or project files.
    • Cookies: To remember login sessions, track progress in courses, and analyze website traffic.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To provide online tuitions and access to academic/non-academic resources.
  • Personalization: Tailoring learning tools to the student’s grade level or interest.
  • Communication: Sending updates regarding class schedules or resources.

3. Data Protection & Children's Privacy

As an EdTech company providing services to children, we take extra precautions. We encourage parental oversight and do not sell student data to third-party advertisers. All online tuition environments are moderated to ensure safety.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access your data, request deletion of your account, or withdraw device permissions (Location/Storage) at any time via your device settings.
